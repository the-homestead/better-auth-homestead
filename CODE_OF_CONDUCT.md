# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must keep project spaces welcoming, constructive, and focused. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and abusive or
demeaning conduct are not accepted.

Examples of positive participation include giving actionable technical feedback, respecting
different experience levels, accepting correction, and prioritizing the health of the community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where
someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. ItzDabbzz may edit or
remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates
this policy. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
